# LinhSinhVN — Project Vision

**Document Version:** 1.0.0  
**Phase:** Phase 0 — Foundation  
**Status:** Approved Initial Baseline  

---

## 1. Project Identity

- **Project Name:** LinhSinhVN (Linh Sinh Việt Nam)
- **Genre:** 2D RPG + Life Simulation + Genetics + Evolution + Survival
- **Perspective:** Top-Down / Isometric 2D creature perspective

### Core Fantasy
The player lives as an authentic creature in the micro-wilderness of Vietnam. The player is **not** a human trainer capturing or commanding pocket monsters. Instead, the player directly embodies the creature itself, fighting for immediate survival, foraging, developing metabolic fitness, finding mates, and creating a persistent biological lineage through genetic recombination, mutation, and environmental adaptation across generations.

---

## 2. Core Loop

The generational loop drives gameplay and biological progression:

```
EXPLORE
   │
   ▼
SURVIVE
   │
   ▼
DEVELOP (Metabolism, Nymph/Larva/Adult Stages)
   │
   ▼
BREED (Mate selection & courtship)
   │
   ▼
RECOMBINE (Allele segregation & crossover)
   │
   ▼
MUTATE (Stochastic baseline & stress-induced variants)
   │
   ▼
PHENOTYPE (Gene expression & morphological manifestation)
   │
   ▼
ADAPT (Niche fitness in changing habitats)
   │
   ▼
SURVIVE (Next generation trials)
   │
   ▼
CONTINUE THE LINEAGE
```

## 3. Core Biological Pipeline

The long-term biological architecture operates on an unbroken chain of causality:

$$\text{GENOME} \longrightarrow \text{GENES} \longrightarrow \text{GENE EXPRESSION} \longrightarrow \text{PHENOTYPE} \longrightarrow \text{STATS} \longrightarrow \text{SURVIVAL} \longrightarrow \text{BREEDING} \longrightarrow \text{RECOMBINATION} \longrightarrow \text{MUTATION} \longrightarrow \text{NEXT GENERATION} \longrightarrow \text{ADAPTATION / EVOLUTION}$$

1. **Genome:** The raw chromosomal data structure inherited from parents.
2. **Genes & Alleles:** Discrete loci encoding traits with dominance, recessiveness, or polygenic modifiers.
3. **Gene Expression:** Environmental conditions and developmental triggers determining active traits.
4. **Phenotype:** Visible anatomy, coloration, size, carapace hardness, appendage forms.
5. **Stats & Mechanics:** Physical speed, venom potency, camouflage index, thermal resistance, metabolic rate.
6. **Survival & Selection:** Fitness testing against predators, climate, starvation, and diseases.
7. **Breeding & Recombination:** Genetic mixing with compatible mates.
8. **Mutation & Evolution:** New genetic variations introduced into the lineage pool.

---

## 4. Core Pillars

1. **Vietnamese Biodiversity:** Deeply rooted in Vietnam’s unique tropical ecosystems—from monsoon rainforests and limestone karsts to terraced fields, mangrove swamps, and urban alleys. Primary focus on indigenous insects, arachnids, and small animals.
2. **Authentic Genetics:** A deterministic, biologically credible genetic model over arbitrary level-up numbers.
3. **Emergent Evolution:** Lineage evolution arises organically from natural selection and genetic drift, rather than pre-scripted linear evolution trees.
4. **Creature-as-Player:** The player experiences the world from the scale, senses, and survival vulnerabilities of the creature.
5. **Ecosystem Survival:** Dynamic food webs, territorial threats, climate seasons, and micro-habitat balances.
6. **Fourth-Wall Humor:** The protagonist creature is self-aware, cognizant of being inside a software simulation running on the player's computer.
7. **Vietnamese Gen-Z Comedy:** Chaotic, satirical, sarcastic dialogue, roasting the player's blunders, and contextually grounded Vietnamese slang and dark humor.
8. **Player-Created Lineage:** The enduring identity of the game is not a single immortal avatar, but the surviving family tree and its adaptations over time.

---

## 5. Character & Narrative Tone

### The Protagonist
- The protagonist is an organism belonging to the lineage.
- It possesses self-awareness and can address the player directly across the fourth wall.
- It remembers player choices, reckless maneuvers, and past deaths in the lineage.
- It frequently insults, critiques, or banters with the player when subjected to poor steering or absurd decisions.
- It utilizes Vietnamese Gen-Z colloquialisms, witty memes, and vulgarity when contextually appropriate.
- **Note:** The dialogue and fourth-wall interactions are an observation and narrative layer; they **must not** control or distort the deterministic simulation engine.

---

## 6. What LinhSinhVN Is NOT

To protect the creative and mechanical integrity of the project, LinhSinhVN will **never** become:
- **A Pokémon clone:** No Pokéballs, no gym badges, no human trainers, no sterile elemental RPS combat.
- **A generic monster collector:** Creatures are living organisms within a phylogenetic tree, not trophies to hoard in digital storage boxes.
- **A generic fantasy RPG:** No mystical mana, spellbooks, or fantasy tropes disconnected from biological realities.
- **A generic pet simulator:** No passive virtual-pet idle loops disconnected from mortality and environmental pressure.

## 7. Concept Classification

### Approved Concepts (Locked Baseline)
- Player directly controls the organism.
- Primary biological focus on Vietnamese biodiversity (insects and small fauna).
- Generational lineage continuity upon death/maturity.
- Deterministic genetic inheritance and phenotypic expression.
- Fourth-wall breaking, sarcastic Vietnamese protagonist voice.
- Data-driven species, trait, and habitat registries.
- Clean separation between core simulation and presentation/dialogue layers.

### Undecided Concepts (Pending Evaluation)
- Specific starting species for the inaugural prototype (e.g., Praying Mantis / Bọ ngựa, Rhinoceros Beetle / Kiến vương, Asian Forest Scorpion / Bọ cạp rừng, or Cicada / Ve sầu).
- Exact 2D artistic rendering technique (Pixel art vs. Hand-drawn 2D vector / Spine vs. High-detail digital illustration).
- Specific chromosomal depth (haploid vs. diploid model, number of chromosome pairs per species).
- Cross-species hybridization boundary conditions.
- Real-time action combat vs. tactical phase-based micro-combat.
- Lineage branching limits per generation.
- Dynamic climate and seasonal weather simulation depth.
- Commercial distribution model and target platforms beyond initial PC desktop.
